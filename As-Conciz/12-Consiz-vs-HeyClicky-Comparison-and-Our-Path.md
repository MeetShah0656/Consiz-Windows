# Consiz vs HeyClicky — Comparison and Our Path
Updated 31 Aug 2026. Companion to files 05 and 09; this is the short, current version.

---

## How Consiz is different

| | HeyClicky | Consiz |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Tutor — "how do I do this?" (arrow points at the button) | Comprehension — "what does this say?" (summary / computed answer) |
| Input | Voice, push-to-talk | Silent middle-click (or hotkey) |
| Engine | Screenshot → vision model (reads pixels) | Reads the actual file/selection; math computed by code |
| Platform | Mac only (Windows waitlist, no date) | Mac today, Windows next — India's home market |
| Price | Free / $20 Pro / $100 Max | ₹289 / ₹499 per month (planned), 14-day trial |

## Who they pitched / their users
- Consumers, not industries: creators and learners stuck in DaVinci, Figma, After Effects; kids making games; doctors with schedules; founders researching. No enterprise sales — pure viral adoption.

## Where they beat us
- Distribution: Farza's Buildspace audience (100k+ builders) → one tweet, ~3M views.
- Money: ~$10.1M (YC Spring 2026 + others).
- Emotional product people share ("a lil buddy next to your cursor").
- Agents: can organize files/calendar, build small Mac apps — broader actions.

## Where we beat them (say "different", never "they can't")
- Windows + India: they are absent from both.
- Silence: voice fails in offices, libraries, shared rooms.
- Real math: they estimate from screenshots; we compute. Design difference, not effort difference.
- Price built for India.

## Ideas to copy
1. 5-second silent demo (messy CSV → click → correct total).
2. Emotional framing, not feature framing.
3. Free download, zero signup for the first taste.
4. Open-source the core (their repo = 7.3k stars of free distribution).
5. Video-first launch: Instagram/X gave them the "insane pull".

## Their YC path → our version
- Theirs: audience → weekend build → viral demo → open source → free download → PH/X/Instagram → YC at 4 weeks old → monetize after.
- Honest read: the engine was the pre-existing audience. Plan for ~5% of their reach.
- Ours: 1) finish Mac build + start Windows, 2) ship the 5-second demo reel (scripted in `reel/`), 3) free-no-signup launch (+ consider open source), 4) push videos into aspirant/CA communities (Telegram, YouTube educators — untouched by them), 5) apply to YC with usage numbers, not documents.
