## Production notes (important)
- AI video tools are bad at readable UI text. Generate the people/mouse/desk shots with AI; record the answer cards yourself (screen recording of the real app) and overlay them. That's what makes it look real.
- Keep the SAME mouse in shot 01 and shot 10 — use the same generated clip, reversed/re-lit.
- One click sound, used 6 times. It becomes the brand sound.
- Captions: big, bottom third, white on a soft black pill, max 5 words per line.
- Deliver a 15 s cut too: shots 01, 03, 04, 06, 10.
